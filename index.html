Northstar Tactical Logistics is a newly established company formed specifically to support the armed forces, whose management and key personnel bring extensive combined experience in the defence logistics sector.

That experience spans maritime port security management, strategic transport, government contracting, port operations, unmanned systems integration and critical infrastructure support. The company is registered under NCAGE 9CZ9H and UEI S9EYKVSLRSQ5 for U.S. and E.U. government contracting.

Northstar is the flagship brand of the NTL Group — a multi-sector holding spanning logistics, energy, maritime, legal and technology, based in Poznań, Poland.

04 — Allied cooperation

NATO COOPERATION

NCAGE: 9CZ9H
UEI: S9EYKVSLRSQ5
U.S. & E.U. REGISTERED

Northstar Tactical Logistics is registered in the NATO codification system under NCAGE 9CZ9H and in the U.S. System for Award Management under UEI S9EYKVSLRSQ5 — the identifiers required to contract with allied defence and government customers. Together with a strategic partner, Northstar prepared a proposal for the United States Transportation Command (USTRANSCOM) covering military logistics through the Port of Gdańsk, with further solutions proposed for the Port of Gdynia.

NATO Codification — NCAGE 9CZ9H

Registered in the NATO codification system, the supplier identifier required to be recognised as a contracting entity by allied defence customers.

Baltic Port Operations

Operational cooperation is being developed with the ports of Gdańsk, Szczecin and Świnoujście, strengthening readiness for military transport, heavy cargo and supply chain coordination.

Host Nation Support

Support offered to allied forces in Poland — fuel, logistics, maintenance, warehousing and administrative services aligned with bilateral Host Nation Support frameworks.

Unmanned Systems

Hybrid UAV platforms, Counter-UAS technology, autonomous systems, AI-assisted observation, MRO and technology transfer, delivered with an experienced European manufacturer.

USTRANSCOM Engagement

Military Equipment Supply
